# Multiplayer mod: visual design pass (2026-10-13, lane uxdesign)

Goal (owner): every mod screen looks and behaves as if the game's own team built it. Same style, clear hierarchy, nothing drawn behind or over other UI, interactions that make sense. Teams window: YOUR units first and prominent, then FREE units, then units you cannot take (other players', reserved) below and muted.

Method: one hidden pair (AB, lease `uxdesign`, `nice -n 10`, 1512x982, `build/dsrig.dylib` of 14:18). Stock popups opened with `ui_teams {action:popup}`, their live hierarchy dumped with `ui_teams {action:dump}` (names, sizes, sprites, TMP font + size; the dumps are in `instances/A/rig.log`, 15:27-15:28), frames by `mpm_shot backbuffer`. Mod states: Teams window (open, taken, marked + guest ready, host admin), HUD, "Owned by" banner. Other mod screens: the 15:0x `fix_*` frames of lane uxgame and the 01-41 frames of the flow review, re-read for style (not re-captured). Colours sampled from the frames (exact pixel values). Pair stopped, lease released, `rig.py check-prefs`: identical. No source edited.

Tags: **RULE** = breaks an owner rule (game style, no overlap / text behind text, hierarchy, game font). **TASTE** = my design judgement; the owner decides. Earlier flow items (ux_review_2026-10-13.md) are not repeated unless the design angle is new.
